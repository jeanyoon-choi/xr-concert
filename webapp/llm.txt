Never use HTTP for device rehearsals. Never emit presentation state from the relay. Never claim the peer-to-peer WebRTC broadcaster is a 100-listener solution.
WebRTC stays minimal and follows `../broadcaster`: admin selects one input and captures one stream; signaling only relays offer/answer/ICE; each listener owns one peer and one audio element. Diagnostics are separate. Do not add mobile audio controls or speculative state machinery.
Do not infer audible success from signaling, ICE, a live track, or `play()`. Follow `docs/harness/webrtc-agent-guardrails.md`: compare against the known-good broadcaster, prove source-meter energy, prove RTP, prove playback, then verify physical audibility.
Camera Collage is a separate P2P video trial. Read `docs/experiments/camera-collage/README.md` before changing it; its signaling is `camera-collage:*` only and the realtime relay must never receive media bytes.
